Hi, my name is Alysa!

I am a software engineer at Pinterest working on real-time systems for user safety.

I'm interested in how people and computers shape the creation and circulation of digital cultural heritage artifacts. I am also very passionate about teaching computational thinking in interdisciplinary settings.

Before that, I graduated from the University of Washington with my BA, BS, and MS degrees, where I was fortunate to discover the exciting world of algorithms, archives and language.

In my free time, I'm probably reading a history book, playing Scrabble, or trying a new taco stand.

I pronounce my name ah-lee-sah (IPA: /əlisə/).
